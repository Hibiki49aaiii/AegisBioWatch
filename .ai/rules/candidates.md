# Candidate Rules

Candidate Rules have stronger support than a single observation but are not yet authoritative guidance.

Promote an Observation here only when at least one of these adds independent support: repeated behavior in separate locations/issues/modules, reproduction in another case, official specification, or a test/implementation result that corroborates the observation.

Each candidate should state: rule, confidence, applicability, verification, exceptions, evidence, and related cases.
