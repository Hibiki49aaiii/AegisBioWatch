# Human Understanding — Issue #12

## What
R301 and R503 are two aligned 47k pull-up resistors. Their +1V8 pads are only 1.500 mm apart and currently appear as the shortest safe discrete unconnected pair in the accepted route-1be DRC evidence.

## Why
Connecting the common +1V8 side is lower-risk than routing either resistor's signal side:
- R301.2 is FLASH_WP_N.
- R503.2 is CHG_PRESENT_N.
- The common rail pads are aligned vertically at x=3.005.
- The two signal pads sit to the right at x=3.645.

## How
Use one 0.30 mm vertical F.Cu track from R301.1 to R503.1. Before adding copper, an executed read-only probe scans the exact standard-rule envelope for unrelated F.Cu pads, tracks, or vias.

## Important decisions
- Do not infer clearance from visual alignment alone.
- Ignore paste-only geometry when evaluating copper blockers.
- Do not route the signal-side pads in this increment.
- Do not add a dogleg or via unless the direct candidate is rejected; in that case defer instead of improvising.

## Failure modes
- Unexpected copper in the direct corridor.
- Signal-pad clearance below rule.
- Ratsnest decrement differs from exactly one.
- Pin/net audit changes.
- Regenerated bytes differ from historical Artifact bytes; this is provenance-only unless electrical/geometry gates fail.

## Change impact
If accepted, routing authority advances from route-1be 0/118/268 to route-1bf 0/117/268. The board remains NOT_FOR_GERBER.
